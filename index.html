About Ma'Moo Worldwide Publications

M

Ma'Moo Worldwide Publications publishes across three areas: novels, self-help guides, and coloring books. Our fiction spans science fiction, climate fiction, hopepunk, romantasy, and hip-hop drama. Our self-help guides are short, structured field guides for readers who suspect a habit of mind is running their life and want a clear, practical way out. Our coloring books offer calm, hand-drawn pages for adults and young readers alike. No affirmations, no fluff, just the work itself.
