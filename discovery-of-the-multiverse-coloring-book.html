About the book

Discovery of the Multiverse

Dive into the world of quantum physics, where intricate illustrations bring quantum experiments, complex geometric shapes, curved manifolds, surfaces and polyhedra to life, and nature wraps around it all. Atoms bloom into flowers, black holes swirl inside mandalas, and polyhedra grow in the garden.

Each page invites you to explore the mysteries of the multiverse while you relax and unwind. A thoughtful gift for science and art lovers of all ages.

  • 40 detailed designs inspired by quantum physics
  • Polyhedra, curved surfaces, mandalas and sacred geometry
  • Flowers, vines and leaves woven through almost every page
  • One-sided pages, so markers won't bleed through
  • A big 8.5 x 11 inch format
